What it is

A layer, not another window to manage.

noDesk sits on and around your desktop. It does the tidying you keep postponing, and shows its work first.

Islands

Files, apps and actions sit in soft panels on the wallpaper layer, behind your windows. Drag them where you like; they snap to a grid and stay put.

The Halo

One small pill at the top of the screen shows the time, your context and short status lines like “Moved 3 files”. Hover it to switch context, undo or open settings.

Command bar

Alt + Space from anywhere. Search, or say what you want: “move pdfs to reading”, “tidy”, “focus 25”. Always shows a preview first.

Edge rails & docks

Stack panels (Inbox, Bins, Launch, Scratch, Projects) on any screen edge as tabs or columns. Icon, label or both, with upright stacked text on vertical rails.

Contexts

Home, Art, Dev, Focus, or your own. Each keeps its own layout and accent colour. Switch with Alt + 1–9. Try them in the demo above.

Shelf & Peek

Drop files on the right-edge Shelf to hold them for later; it only keeps references. Press Space on any file for a quick preview.

A smart Inbox

Watches Desktop and Downloads (off until you turn it on), suggests a bin for each new file with a reason, and tidies the sure things on its own.

Desk health

Finds stale, duplicate and huge files and near-duplicate images, and offers an archive plan. Installer and archive clean-up included.

Smart rename & rules

Names from a PDF’s title, a photo’s date or where a download came from. Rule suggestions come from your own moves, so noDesk learns your habits, not someone else’s.

Smart, but local, and no AI. Everything below runs on your PC with plain rules and file metadata. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Calm by design

Show first. Move second. Undo always.

  1. PreviewTidy, rules and the command bar all show exactly what will move where, with a reason and a confidence. Low confidence says “needs you” instead of guessing.
  2. JournalEvery applied change is written to a local journal, so noDesk can always tell you what it did and when.
  3. UndoOne shared Ctrl + Z across every move, from any panel.

Archive only

noDesk never deletes. Old and duplicate files move to an archive you can browse and restore from.

Local only

No account, no cloud AI, no telemetry. The only network traffic is the update check.

Easy in, easy out

A per-user installer that needs no admin rights. Uninstalling leaves your files and bins exactly where they are.
